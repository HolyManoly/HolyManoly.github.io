I helped bring this game to life with Seattle Indie Game Creatives, a group of game dev hobbyists that met through Meetup. We would gather on weekends in a small coffee shop, brainstorm and test out different quirky ideas, until getting one that sticked. Enter S.L.U.R.P., a quirky little mobile puzzle game that definitely had a bunch of interesting design challenges. I was in charge of the full initial Unity implementation, built up a WebGL level editor for the whole team to use, punching holes in game mechanic design and then brainstorm design ideas about how to patch those holes up, or take a completely different route.
